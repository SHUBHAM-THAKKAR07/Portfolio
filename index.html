🏆 1st Place — Critical Thinking Challenge

Flood-Resilient Smart City — Case Study

A live problem-solving round with three escalating twists. Starting scenario: heavy rainfall floods a smart city, knocking out roads, communication, and power — with judges invalidating our solution and adding a new constraint every 20 minutes across 3 rounds.

  • Underground diversion drainage with sensor + manual dual-mode caps that auto-open under excess water weight, routing floodwater to storage tanks
  • In-path micro-turbines that generate electricity from the diverted flow itself
  • An offline, SMS-based communication layer for public updates when networks fail
  • Automated traffic clearance triggered by the same flood-detection system
Role: Team Lead Event: Shantilal Shah College, Bhavnagar
